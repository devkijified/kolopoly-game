# Kolopoly play lessons (owner + Muse live session, 2026-10-27)
Lessons captured while playing together, to become the next improvement round.

1. LOBBY ROOM DIES WITH THE HOST'S TAB (owner, 00:03): he created a room, closed his browser ~2 minutes, reopened, and got "your room don expire"; the invite link died with it. Seat reclaim covers in-game seats, but a pre-start lobby room is destroyed when the host's socket closes. Fix: lobby grace window (~10 min) where the room and its code persist without the host, guests see "host step out small, room go wake when e return", and the host reclaims the lobby on return exactly like an in-game seat.

2. ROTATE SHRINKS THE BOARD (owner, on his phone, 2026-10-27 00:05): "rotate didn't do anything special to the board, it even shrinked." In landscape the layout splits board-left + rail-right and the square board is fitted to the short viewport height, so it renders SMALLER than the portrait follow view that fills the width zoomed. The "rotate for the full board" hint therefore keeps a false promise. Fix directions: landscape gets the follow/zoom view too (completeness is one tap away via Full board), chrome slims in landscape (compact phase strip, smaller header) so the stage grows, and the hint copy stops promising size it cannot give.
3. JOIN FLOW SEAT/NAME TANGLE (same session): the joining browser carried a stale saved test profile ("TesterTwo", Danfo) from earlier verification sessions; the first join grabbed that seat, the host started, and the later "Join as Muse" tap reclaimed the SAME seat by player ID (working as designed) but under the wrong name and token, with no way to correct identity from inside. Fix directions: when a join prompt's remembered name/token differs from an existing seat in that room, say so on the prompt ("you get one seat here as TesterTwo") and offer "use that seat" vs "switch name next game"; never silently present two identities for one seat.
4. THE ROLL BUTTON IS A MOVING TARGET (same session, 00:08): the per-second countdown lives INSIDE the Roll dice button's label, so the label mutates every second. Human thumbs do not care, but the moment anything (automation, assistive tech, a shaky hand mid-tap) needs a stable target, the main action of the game is the least stable element on screen. Improvement: freeze the primary label ("Roll dice" never changes) and move the countdown to its own chip beside it. Credit where due: the game's own timeout machinery carried the seat gracefully (auto-roll on timeout rolled 5+5, card resolved, auto-end passed the turn), so a stalled player never freezes the room; that part is validated, not a defect.
5. PLAYERS IN PARALLEL (owner's order, mid-game, 2026-10-27 00:10: "write the players in parallel"): management leaves the turn. Any player may build, mortgage, unmortgage, trade and make deals AT ANY TIME, even during another player's turn; only the dice, landing resolution, auctions and card draws stay serial to the turn player. This is true to the table game (building and trading were never turn-locked in the parlour) and it deletes the dead waiting that this session exposed: while one seat thinks, every other seat is working. Server notes for the build: management intents must validate against live state (cash, ownership, building evenness) at execution, not at click; a management action that would collide with the active landing resolution (e.g. mortgaging the street being charged) resolves in message order with clear feed lines; build controls surface in the rail for non-turn players with a quiet "you fit build anytime" hint.
6. FULL BOARD ON A PORTRAIT PHONE IS A POSTAGE STAMP (owner's screenshot, iPhone Safari, 2026-10-27 00:11): the full-board view on his phone renders the board at roughly half the screen with dark stage margins on every side and unreadable tiles. The framing fit was tuned on the desktop landscape stage; portrait full view needs its own fit computed from the live stage aspect so the board fills ~97% of stage width, centred, in both orientations. Companion fixes: (a) on phones the default view is Follow at all times (including while spectating another player's turn) unless the player explicitly picked Full, and the pick is remembered; (b) turn banner copy leak: his phone read "Na your turn, TesterTwo" during MY turn; the second-person form must render only on the turn player's own client, spectators get "Na TesterTwo turn"; (c) the centre bridge still splits the wordmark in full view (KOLO / POLY), the known centre debt, now photographed on a real device.
7. TIMER MUST BEEP BEFORE IT BITES (owner's order, 2026-10-27 00:12): when the turn timer enters its last 10 seconds (owner corrected 5 to 10 on the spot), beep once per second (soft ticks, firmer in the final 3, short final beep at zero) so a distracted player looks back before the auto-action takes their turn. Same treatment already exists for auction closes; unify the pattern across turn timer, deal/choice clocks and Kirikiri roll prompts. Pair each beep with a small pulse of the timer chip. Mute governs it like every other sound; in Inflation the Phase horn stays the phase signal, the beeps are per-turn only.
8. TOKENS GET LOST ON THE BOARD (player-seat observation, 2026-10-27 00:13): at board distance the 3D tokens are small enough that a player confirms landings from the banner text, not from seeing the piece. Fixes: the active player's token gets a pulsing player-colour halo and a gentle scale-up during its move; the landing tile flashes its colour band on arrival; in Follow view the camera sits a touch lower and closer so the moving token fills more of the frame. Praise logged from the same seat: the card wording and salary/Wahala payouts "feel lively and local"; the voice layer is landing as designed, do not flatten it in future copy passes.
9. PAYMENT RECEIVED MUST SOUND LIKE OPAY, AND OPAY SPEAKS (owner: "isn't well mimiced, check online for opay like sound"; researched 2026-10-27): the finding is that OPay's payment-received moment is famous for its VOICE alert: the app announces "Payment received in OPay" out loud (free, toggleable; merchants and POS agents across Nigeria run it, users describe it as shouting). Our credit moment has the green check visual but only a plain two-tone ding, so it reads as a generic alert, not money arriving. Round spec: credits gain (a) a brighter three-note success chime, still synthesized, never a sampled OPay asset; (b) a spoken announcement in our own words via speech synthesis on the receiving player's device only: "Payment received, {amount}" plus "from {name}" on rent, e.g. "Payment received, ₦100,000 from Tife." It respects mute, is host-toggleable, supersedes cleanly when a newer credit lands, and degrades silently to chime-only where the browser blocks speech before a user gesture. Debits never speak.
10. WHICH GAME AM I IN? (owner, mid-game: "i don't see the Inflation timer, or is this not inflation?"): this room was Classic and nothing on the game screen says so persistently; the phase bar exists only in Inflation, and Classic's 60-minute match clock has no equally glanceable home. Fix: a permanent mode pill in the top bar ("Classic · 60 min" / "Inflation · Phase 2"), and the match clock counts down in the same rail slot the phase bar occupies in Inflation, so remaining time is always visible in both modes.
11. NO WAY TO ABANDON A GAME (owner: "can player abandon game?"): today there is no leave or resign control; closing the tab just pauses the room and strands the other seats. Fix: a Leave game control in the game menu with an honest confirm ("You wan give up this game?"). Two players: leaving is a concession, the other player wins and the final table records "won by resignation". Three or four players: the leaver's cash goes to the bank, buildings clear, deeds return to the bank for resale, and the game continues without them. The host additionally gets "End room for everyone". A resigned seat never lingers as a ghost.
12. FOLLOW BOARD / FOLLOW TOKEN FEELS DEAD (owner, on his phone, 2026-10-27 00:17): the view toggle "not responding effectively". Contributing causes to fix together with lesson 6: (a) in portrait the two views are too similar in framing, so a successful switch reads as no response; make Follow a tight crop on the token's side (about 2.2x the full-board zoom) and Full the fitted whole board, a difference nobody can miss; (b) taps during a token move animation are effectively ignored because the camera is locked to the move path; the toggle must interrupt the animation camera immediately; (c) the response itself must be instant and obvious: act on pointerdown, glide in about 300ms, flash the button's active state; hit area at least 44px and never overlapped by the rotate-hint pill or turn banner. Verification for the round: physical tap test in the rig at 390x844, toggle pressed mid-animation and at rest, frame-diffed so the two states are provably distinct.
13. THE FEED MUST CONFESS ITS AUTO ACTIONS (player-seat observation, 2026-10-27 00:22): when a timeout roll and a manual roll both appear in the game gist, a player cannot reconstruct which roll produced a card or a landing. Fix: every server auto-action (timeout roll, auto-end, forced payment) carries an unmistakable "(auto)" tag and its reason in the feed line, so the history of a turn can be read back exactly.
14. THE PAUSE STATE WHISPERS WHEN IT SHOULD SPEAK (live event, 2026-10-27 00:29): when the host's connection dropped mid-game, the remaining client got a toast ("The game is paused, waiting for players") and a Roll button frozen at 0s that accepted clicks and did nothing. A paused game needs a full, honest waiting card over the board: who we are waiting for by name, how long we have waited, that the turn clock is frozen and at what value, and every action control visibly disabled rather than silently inert. Silent dead buttons teach players the game is broken; a named wait teaches them it is holding their place.
